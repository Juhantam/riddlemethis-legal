Privacy Policy — Riddle me this

Last updated: 19 August 2026

“Riddle me this” (the “app”) is an Android riddle game published by Juhan Tamm (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it.

The short version

  • Your game progress (solved riddles, hints, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your device. We run no servers and cannot see it.
  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The only third party that processes data is Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the app free. In the European Economic Area, ads only start after you make a consent choice.
  • Buying “Remove ads” is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device only

The app saves your progress (which riddles you solved, attempts, unlocked hints and packs, riddle-of-the-day history) and your settings (notification preferences, purchase status) in the app’s private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When ads are served, Google may collect and process, among other things:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app interactions with ads, diagnostic data).

This data is collected and used by Google to serve and measure ads, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising partner disclosures.

Your consent (EEA/UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested, using Google’s consent framework. The legal basis for personalized advertising is your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app. If you decline, the ads SDK is not started.

Purchases

The optional one-time “Remove ads” purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive no payment information. The purchase state is stored on your device and can be restored through Google Play on a new device.

Notifications

The optional daily riddle notification is scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service is used, and no data leaves your device for it.

Children

The app is intended for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children.

Data retention and deletion

We retain nothing, because we store nothing on servers. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. For data processed by Google (advertising), see Google’s My Ad Center and Privacy Policy for your controls and rights.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection regarding personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, these rights are in practice exercised toward Google for advertising data (links above). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in Estonia: Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

Changes

If a future version of the app adds features that change what data is handled (for example an optional cloud save), this policy will be updated before that version is released, with the date above revised.
